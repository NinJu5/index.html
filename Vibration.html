Vibration Game Jam

A two-day game creation challenge

Contexte

A Game Jam project where the theme was "Vibration". We had two days to create a complete game as a team of two students. This project was created during my studies at Brassart Toulouse.

Rôle

Programmer
Level Designer
Sound Designer

Durée

2 jours
Game Jam Format

Équipe

2 étudiants
Brassart Toulouse

À propos du projet

Vibration Game Jam was an intense creative experience where we had to develop a complete game around the "Vibration" theme. Within a tight 48-hour window, our team of two managed to create a playable game that explores the concept of vibration as both a mechanical and narrative element.

As the programmer, I handled the core gameplay mechanics and game architecture. My level design work focused on creating engaging challenges that integrate the vibration mechanic meaningfully. Additionally, I contributed sound design elements to enhance the player experience.

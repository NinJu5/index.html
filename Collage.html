Collage Game Jam

Typography and narrative as gameplay mechanics

Contexte

A Game Jam project created during a Narrative Design class. The objective was to experiment with fonts and writing styles as a core gameplay mechanic. An innovative approach to integrating narrative design directly into the gameplay experience.

Rôle

Narrative Designer
Game Designer
Level Designer

Durée

1 semaine
Class Assignment

Focus

Narrative Design
Typography & Writing

À propos du projet

Collage Game Jam was a unique project that pushed the boundaries of narrative design in game development. The core concept was to use fonts and writing styles not just as visual elements, but as fundamental gameplay mechanics that directly affect the player's experience and understanding of the story.

This project taught me how to integrate storytelling directly into the mechanics, making narrative design inseparable from gameplay design. By treating typography and writing style as interactive elements, we created a game where the way the story is presented is as important as the story itself.

The experience reinforced my understanding of how narrative design can enhance gameplay, and how limitations (in this case, focusing on typography) can lead to creative innovation.
